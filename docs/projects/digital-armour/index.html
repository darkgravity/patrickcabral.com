Digital Armour

PracticeDigital motion studies
ArtistPatrick Cabral
SeriesTechsugi

Dark fitted armour and a segmented head explore the boundary between a body and a constructed surface. Reflective plates, narrow seams, and accents of light give the figures a mechanical character.

The Robo head demonstrates a sci-fi panel generator built in Houdini to simplify hard-surface modelling, with clean topology intended for use in games. Patrick cited MGXS as an inspiration for this experiment. It appears here alongside a separate fitted-armour study from the Techsugi archive.
