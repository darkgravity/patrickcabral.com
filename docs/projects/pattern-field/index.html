Topographical Beads

PracticeAlgorithmic beadwork
ArtistPatrick Cabral
SeriesTechsugi

Philippine landscapes translated into digital beadwork. Geographical contours and elevation lines become thousands of densely arranged, coloured spheres, giving the shape of the land a tactile, patterned presence.

The work connects cartography with the careful repetition of traditional beadwork. The landscape becomes a crafted surface, inviting the viewer to read its ridges, valleys, and changing elevations through colour and detail.
