Lab / Grown

Digital explorations

Grown

PracticeAlgorithmic garment study
ArtistPatrick Cabral
SeriesTechsugi

A digital garment imagined as something that grows around the body. Inspired by the intricate pathways of mycelial networks, its details gather into a silhouette with the character of a living organism.

Growth becomes a way of thinking about structure. The study explores how individual forms can spread, connect, and accumulate around a figure, creating a garment that appears to have bloomed into shape.
