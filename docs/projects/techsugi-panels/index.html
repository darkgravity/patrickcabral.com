Modern Patterns

PracticeHoudini · Manhattan Voronoi patterns
ArtistPatrick Cabral
SeriesTechsugi

An evolution of Patrick’s earlier weaving experiments, Modern Patterns reimagines textile structures through the geometry of Manhattan Voronoi. Interlocking shapes draw on the visual language of science fiction, skyscrapers, and circuit boards.

The work is inspired by the T’boli Dreamweavers and the stories carried through their textiles. Growing up alongside rapid technological change, Patrick draws from the images and structures of his own era to explore how a personal story might take the form of a pattern.

Studies in motion

Version 2 · A digital loom

The second version uses dynamic tessellation to translate an image or video into an intricate pattern in real time. Acting as a digital loom, the system continually rebuilds its structure in response to the incoming imagery. This still demonstrates the process through a reinterpretation of Girl with a Pearl Earring.
