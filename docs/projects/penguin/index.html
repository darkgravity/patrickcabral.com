Penguin Books

PracticeLayered paper
ArtistPatrick Cabral
CommissionPenguin Books

Commissioned by Paul Buckley for Penguin Books’ New York headquarters, a white penguin sits within a deep field of warm, stepped colour. The project connects Patrick’s paper practice with a publisher whose books helped shape his curiosity as a child.

Patrick had long admired Buckley’s work, including his collaborations with Alex Trochut. The commission also brought back memories of a childhood Random House encyclopedia, through which Patrick encountered subjects such as Albrecht Dürer and Frank Lloyd Wright’s Fallingwater. Finding the right background was a separate challenge: Patrick and Camy repeatedly repainted its layers, mixing a difficult gradient until the colours gave the white figure enough contrast. Shared in July 2019, the piece records both a personal connection to books and the patient work behind its apparently simple colour transitions.
