Our Lady of Peñafrancia

PracticeLayered paper sculpture
ArtistPatrick Cabral

A layered paper interpretation of Our Lady of Peñafrancia, affectionately called Ina. Created for Patrick’s friends Sweet and Joseph, the piece celebrates a milestone in their lives while holding close the faith, friendships, and sense of home that shaped them.

Patrick and Joseph’s friendship reaches back to childhood, when they served together as altar boys. They later shared a home while studying in Naga City, and again when they began working in Manila. Delivered to Joseph and Sweet as they built their life in Dallas, the sculpture became a reminder of where they came from. When it was unpacked, several of those present were also devotees of Ina, turning the occasion into an unexpected moment of shared devotion.
