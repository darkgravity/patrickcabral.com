Generative Footwear

PracticeHoudini · generative fashion
ArtistPatrick Cabral
SeriesTechsugi

A sculptural shoe built from clustered geometric volumes. Colour separates the growing forms as they wrap around the upper and extend into a segmented sole.

Created with a custom three-dimensional Manhattan Voronoi generator in Houdini. Patrick developed the workflow while exploring generative fashion, replacing the repeated resculpting that changes to 3D-printing designs had required in ZBrush with a more adaptable procedural approach.
