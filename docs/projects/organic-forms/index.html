Organic Forms

PracticeDigital study
ArtistPatrick Cabral
SeriesTechsugi

Flowing white forms with deep openings and layered surface rhythms. Seen from multiple angles, the continuous outer shell breaks into folds, channels, and porous spaces.

The Bone image sequence in the Techsugi archive records the form through changing viewpoints. These selected views extend the original two studies, revealing how the openings and overlapping surfaces change around the object.
