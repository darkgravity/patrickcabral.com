Philippine Weave AR

PracticeAugmented reality · algorithmic weaving
ArtistPatrick Cabral
SeriesTechsugi

An augmented reality experiment that transforms live video into patterns inspired by traditional Philippine weaving. As people move in front of the camera, their presence becomes part of a continuously changing digital textile.

The audience becomes an active part of the artwork. Faces, gestures, and movement become threads, bringing the people in the room into a shared, unfolding interpretation of Philippine culture.
