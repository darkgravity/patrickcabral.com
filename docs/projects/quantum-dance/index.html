Textile Physics

PracticeHoudini · procedural textiles
ArtistPatrick Cabral
SeriesTechsugi

An experiment in making digital textiles easier to art-direct, from the shape of a fold to the way a surface moves. Fabric becomes a material for choreography, guided as deliberately as a sculptor shapes a surface.

The accompanying film, Quantum Dance, demonstrates a Houdini system that develops fabric-like forms from an edge or path. Noise remapping drives the motion, while curvature controls the appearance of creases. The study explores how an artist can guide textile form and behaviour with greater control.
