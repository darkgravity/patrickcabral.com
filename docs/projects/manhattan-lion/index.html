Geometric Lion

PracticeDigital form study
ArtistPatrick Cabral
SeriesTechsugi

A lion-like face built from interlocking, stepped planes. Bright colour separates the angular pieces, while a monochrome version brings the structure and depth of the relief into focus.

Full views, a close detail, and a three-version comparison from the Techsugi archive show how colour and surface treatment change the same geometric language.
