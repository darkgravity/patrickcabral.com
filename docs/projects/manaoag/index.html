Our Lady of Manaoag

PracticeLayered paper sculpture
ArtistPatrick Cabral

A layered paper interpretation of Our Lady of Manaoag, holding the Child Jesus beneath an ornate golden crown and halo. Deep blue, plum, and gold frame the figures, while curling foliage and a layered flower surround them with intricate ornament.

Lace-like cutwork runs through the robes, halo, and foliage. Stacked paper creates depth through real edges and shadows, with details extending from the faces and crowns to the rosary and the flower at the base of the composition.
