Sculpture

Cha Long Wolves

PracticePainted layered plywood
ArtistPatrick Cabral
CommissionMaxim’s Hong Kong / Cha Long

Commissioned by Maxim’s Hong Kong for its Cha Long brand, these wolf characters are built from laser-cut plywood. Layered faces, expressive clothing, and carefully painted details turn the brand’s characters into dimensional portraits.

The project adapts the visual language of Patrick’s paper work to wood. Patrick handled sanding while Camy painted the layers, a division of work that brought the complex construction and colour together. The second wolf’s hat alone used roughly twenty colours. The finished characters combine the precision of laser cutting with the patient surface work required to give each painted layer its own presence.
