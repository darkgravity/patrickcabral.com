Works / Starbucks Reserve

Sculpture

Starbucks Reserve

PracticeLayered MDF
ArtistPatrick Cabral
CommissionStarbucks Reserve

Three animals. Three coffee-growing regions. A collection of intricately layered sculptures created for Starbucks Reserve at SM North Towers, Quezon City. The Sumatran tiger, Kenyan elephant, and Guatemalan quetzal translate the origins of coffee into pattern, texture, and relief.

Created for the Reserve store at SM North Towers. This installation translates the visual language of paper cutting into layered MDF for a lasting architectural display.
