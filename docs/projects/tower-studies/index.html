Tower Studies

PracticeDigital award concept
ArtistPatrick Cabral

An award concept built around the letter T in a client’s logo. Stacked, shifting profiles turn the letter into two slender skyscraper forms, exploring how a graphic identity might become a sculptural object.

The original request was for a certificate; Patrick proposed a trophy instead. Paper was the intended material, but the images document the design study rather than a manufactured award. The concept uses repetition and changing outlines to move between a recognisable letter, an architectural silhouette, and an object that could mark an achievement.
