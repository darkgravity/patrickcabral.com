Works / Royal Mint · Money Heist

Sculpture

Royal Mint · Money Heist

PracticeLayered paper
ArtistPatrick Cabral
CommissionNetflix Philippines

Created with Netflix Philippines for Money Heist, this paper interpretation of the Royal Mint of Spain turns a symbol of institutional power into something delicate. Its white façade is built from intricate layers, with ornamental detail inspired by the engraved lines of banknotes.

Patrick chose the Royal Mint rather than the series’ familiar Dalí mask because architecture offered a new challenge. The building represents the system at the heart of the story; rendering it in fragile paper creates a tension between authority and vulnerability. Published in September 2021, the project brings the precision of architectural relief together with the visual language of currency. Detail views reveal the columns, windows, and decorative edges that give the façade its depth.
