Works / Honkai Impact 3rd

Sculpture

Honkai Impact 3rd

PracticeLayered paper
ArtistPatrick Cabral
CommissionHonkai Impact 3rd

A commissioned artwork for Honkai Impact 3rd’s fourth-anniversary giveaway, this figure translates a game character into approximately fifty layers of paper. White hair, armour, a sword, and a sweeping red cape bring the character’s movement into a dimensional composition.

This was Patrick’s first full-bodied human figure in layered paper, extending a practice previously centred on animals and ornamental forms. The commission also had a personal connection: the character was a favourite of his niece. Patrick recorded the making process, showing how many separate paper layers come together to create the pose, costume, and depth of the finished work.
