Rosa

PracticeDigital motion studies
ArtistPatrick Cabral
SeriesTechsugi

Bright ribbons open and fold into a flower-like form. The shifting surfaces move between petal, fabric, and abstract sculpture.

Named Rosa in the Techsugi archive, the motion study uses contrasting colour to make each curling surface legible as the form changes. The dark background isolates the changing silhouette.
