Mananayaw ng Langit at Lupa

PracticeArchival coloured paper
ArtistPatrick Cabral
CommissionEdwin Valencia for ILOMOCA

Dancers of Heaven and Earth: two masks made from archival coloured paper for the Dinagyang Festival in 2021. The blue bird, Langit, evokes air, flight, horizons, and dreams. The red and yellow dragon or buwaya, Lupa, brings together fire, earth, light, and passion.

Commissioned by Edwin Valencia for the Iloilo Museum of Contemporary Art, the work is recorded at 32 × 38 inches. The paired figures explore duality through colour, movement, and layers of ornament. Their imagery draws together faith, trade, and worship, giving each mask a distinct character while allowing them to form a single conversation between the earthly and the celestial.
